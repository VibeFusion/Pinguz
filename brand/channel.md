# Channel kit — Jury's In

## Name
**Jury's In** (recommended). Handle: @jurysin (fallbacks: @jurysinstories, @thejurysin).
Why: every video ends on "was I wrong?", and the verdict end card is the channel's signature.
The name promises the audience a job (judge the story) and gives the host a format hook
("the jury's in" = the verdict card). Short, two words, no numbers, reads at 48 px.

Alternatives considered:
- **The Last Line** — every story turns on its final sentence; quieter, more literary.
- **Hold That Thought** — from the v4 story's "Hold." note; warmer, harder to say fast.

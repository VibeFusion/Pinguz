  HTTPS goes through an agent proxy; the CA bundle is at

  `/root/.ccr/README.md` and `curl -sS "$HTTPS_PROXY/__agentproxy/status"`.
- **Never commit `.env`.** `.env.example` lists every variable.
- **No model identifiers** in commit messages, PR titles or bodies, code
  comments, or anything else pushed to the repo. Chat replies only.
- Commit footer:
  `Co-Authored-By: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` plus the `Claude-Session:`

## 3. What the channel is

**Jury's In** (`@jurysin`) — 9:16 shorts, one original story per video, narrated
over oddly-satisfying AI footage with word-by-word karaoke captions. Every story
ends on a question rather than an answer, then a 3 s verdict card carries the
host's one-line take. The audience's job is to deliver the verdict in the
comments.

Publishing order: YouTube Shorts first, then Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook,
Snapchat, Reddit. Per-platform limits live in `src/factory/platforms.py`;
upload-API notes are in `docs/PUBLISHING.md`.

Stories are **original fiction from the script agent — never scraped from
Reddit.** That is a hard format rule, not a preference.

**Title-card style is indifferent.** Box, outline and none all scored the same;
don't spend another A/B on it.

**Script lengths** live in `story.LENGTHS`, not the CLI: short 120–140 words
(≈45–50 s, for Shorts and Reels), long 175–200 words (≈65–75 s, clears TikTok's
60 s Creator Rewards floor). Change them there.

**Every script carries a `verdict`.** `factory make` renders it as a 3 s end card
after the narration with the voice track padded (`apad`) so the music bed keeps
playing; `factory export` adds a "My take:" line to captions. This is the
creator-perspective layer YouTube's 2025 inauthentic-content policy asks for.

**Backgrounds never carry narrative** — no faces, no text, continuous motion.

## 6. Policy and monetisation research

**AI disclosure.** YouTube requires the "altered or synthetic content" label
only when the content could be mistaken for real — a cloned real person's voice,
altered real events, a real person appearing to say something they didn't.
Generic AI narration of clearly fictional stories does not trigger it. The
channel description says the stories are original fiction narrated with an AI
voice anyway, which is the honest position and costs nothing. TikTok requires an
AI label for realistic AI-generated visuals or audio and auto-detects C2PA
metadata, so label there.

**What pays.** Shorts RPM by niche: finance $0.08–0.35 per 1k views,
entertainment and storytelling $0.02–0.07. TikTok Creator Rewards pays
$0.40–1.50 per 1k but only on videos over 60 s, which is why the long script
length exists. Long-form YouTube pays 50–100× Shorts on the same view count.
The honest read: a story channel monetises through volume, audience transfer to
long-form, and eventually brand deals, not Shorts RPM.

## 9. Tooling notes that save time

**Higgsfield CDN is blocked from the container.** Both
`d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net` (output) and `d2ol7oe51mr4n9.cloudfront.net`
(input) are unreachable locally. Workarounds that worked:

- Render inside `sandbox_exec` rather than downloading. The sandbox has Pillow,
  faster-whisper, Montserrat, ffmpeg and internet access. Its tool timeout is
  60 s, so run anything longer as a background job.
- Read predictor results by scraping the dashboard HTML with a regex on
  `overall_score`.
- View a generated image by having the sandbox downscale it and emit base64,
  then decode locally. At full size the base64 gets truncated; 128 px at q45
  fits.
- Upload via `media_upload` presigned PUT then `media_confirm`.
  `generate_image_batch` with `gpt_image_2_5` produced the icons.

**ElevenLabs Scribe returns text only, no word timestamps.** For caption timings
the v4 build ran faster-whisper (`small`, int8) in the Higgsfield sandbox and
merged split currency tokens (`"$2"` + `",000"` → `"$2,000"`).

**`factory` console script is not always on `PATH`.** Use
`python3 -m factory.cli`.

**Pass absolute paths to `--bank`.** ffmpeg runs in the ASS directory;
`assemble.render()` now resolves clip paths, but relative bank paths were the
cause of a "No such file" bug.
